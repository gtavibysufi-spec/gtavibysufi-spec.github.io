FAQ

Good questions.

Still unsure? Send a message and I will answer the same day.

How long does a project take?

A logo usually takes 3 to 5 days, a brand identity 7 to 10 days, a short video 2 to 4 days and an explainer or campaign film 1 to 3 weeks. You get an exact date with your quote.

How do revisions work?

Each package lists its revision rounds. A round is one consolidated set of feedback. Extra rounds are $25 each, and small tweaks are never counted as a round.

Which files will I receive?

Logos come as SVG, PNG and PDF. Print work comes as press-ready PDF. Videos are delivered as MP4 in the sizes you need, such as 16:9, 9:16 and 1:1. Source files (AI, PSD, Figma, AEP) are available as an add-on.

How does payment work?

50% upfront to book your slot and 50% on approval, before final files are released. Retainers are billed monthly in advance.

Do you do websites or software development?

No. I design interfaces and websites in Figma and hand them over for your developer to build, but I do not write code or build apps.
